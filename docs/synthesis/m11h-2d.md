# M11h — 2D strip transfer for the decay algebra

**M-id:** M11h · **Status:** **Existence proved + strip transfer (numeric)** — 2D plaquette lattice
**Date:** 2026-07-30 · **Stance:** Preliminary research; finite/algorithmic rigor, **not** a continuum theorem.
**Generalizes:** [m11g-decay-algebra-general-w-theorem.md](m11g-decay-algebra-general-w-theorem.md) (1D, any gate/width)
**Witness:** `simulations/classical/m11_decay_algebra_2d.py`

Lifts the decay algebra to a 2D lattice. The boundary becomes a **row-cut of width \(W\)** and the transfer becomes an operator on the \(2^W\) cut configurations — the Bayesian-filter form of a statistical-mechanics **strip transfer matrix**. This is the last discrete-structure rung; it also exposes the exponential wall that motivates the continuum embedding.

---

## 1. Setup

I.i.d. fair grid \(b_{ij}\). 2×2-plaquette AND:


$$
Y_{ij} = b_{i,j}\wedge b_{i,j+1}\wedge b_{i+1,j}\wedge b_{i+1,j+1}.
$$


Per-site export density \(a := \lim E/\#\text{sites}\), where \(E=H(X\mid Y)=(\#\text{input bits})-H(Y)\).

**Row reduction.** Read the grid row by row. The hidden state is the current **input row** \(R_i\in\{0,1\}^{W}\); the fresh input is the next row \(R_{i+1}\) (uniform, independent); the emission is the **output row** \(\mathbf Y_i=(Y_{i,0},\dots,Y_{i,W-2})\in\{0,1\}^{W-1}\), a function of \((R_i,R_{i+1})\). So the output-row process is a hidden Markov chain on the \(2^{W}\)-state row alphabet.

---

## 2. Existence (proved)

\((Y_{ij})\) is a **finite-range function of an i.i.d. field** on \(\mathbb Z^2\): plaquettes not sharing a vertex use disjoint bits, so the field is finitely dependent, stationary and ergodic. Its per-site entropy rate \(h_{2D}=\lim_{n}H(Y_{\text{block }n})/n^2\) exists (subadditive/Fekete argument for stationary fields on \(\mathbb Z^d\)). Since the input-bit count is one per site up to boundary, the density


$$
a_{2D} \;=\; \lim \frac{E}{\#\text{sites}}
$$


**exists**. \(\square\) *(Rigor: proved, same finite-dependence mechanism as 1D, now for the \(\mathbb Z^2\) action.)*

---

## 3. Strip transfer at width \(W\) (proved via m11g)

Fix strip width \(W\). The output-row chain is a function of the i.i.d. row process \((R_i)\), i.e. exactly the general-\(w\) situation of [m11g](m11g-decay-algebra-general-w-theorem.md) with the **row** \(R_i\) as the super-symbol and a **vector** emission \(\mathbf Y_i\). Therefore:

- the per-row entropy rate \(h_{\text{row}}^{(W)}\) exists and admits the **Blackwell representation** \(h_{\text{row}}^{(W)}=\mathbb E_{\mu^\star_W}[H(\mathbf Y\mid\pi)]\), with \(\mu^\star_W\) the invariant belief measure of the transfer operator on the \(2^{W}\) row-cut;
- the **width-\(W\) per-site density** is


$$
a_W \;=\; \frac{W - h_{\text{row}}^{(W)}}{\,W-1\,}
$$


(input rate \(W\) bits per row, minus the output-row entropy, per \(W-1\) output sites).

*(Rigor: proved — direct instance of m11g with a product alphabet. The reset/regeneration corollary weakens: an all-ones output row is the only reset and is exponentially rare, so \(\mu^\star_W\) is effectively non-atomic and the operator is evaluated by Monte-Carlo filtering.)*

Convergence \(a_W \to a_{2D}\) as \(W\to\infty\) is standard for strip densities (numerical here).

---

## 4. Numerical witness

`m11_decay_algebra_2d.py` — strip transfer (MC belief filter) vs exact strip enumeration:

| \(W\) | \(2^{W}\) states | \(a_{\text{enum}}\) | \(a_{\text{transfer}}\) | \(\lvert\Delta\rvert\) |
|------|------------------|---------------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| 2 | 4 | 1.68412 | 1.68429 | \(1.7\times10^{-4}\) |
| 3 | 8 | 1.19251 | 1.19329 | \(7.8\times10^{-4}\) |
| 4 | 16 | — (too large) | 1.03098 | — |

Width convergence: \(a_2,a_3,a_4 = 1.684,\,1.193,\,1.031\); increments \(-0.491,\,-0.162\) (shrinking \(\approx3\times\) ⇒ converging, but slowly). Densities exceed 1 because at width \(W\) there are \(W/(W-1)\) input bits per site; as \(W\to\infty\) this \(\to1\) and \(a_W\) decreases toward the bulk value.

---

## 5. The wall (why continuum is next)

The row-cut has \(2^{W}\) states and \(2^{W-1}\) emission branches, so **both enumeration and the transfer operator cost grow exponentially in strip width**. We cannot pin \(a_{2D}\) precisely this way. This is not a defect of the method — it is the generic obstruction of discrete transfer in \(d\ge2\), and it is exactly why the **continuum embedding** (take the limit; obtain a density field directly) is the necessary next rung rather than an optional one.

---

## 6. Scope, rigor, non-claims

| Statement | Rigor |
|-----------|-------|
| 2D per-site density \(a_{2D}\) exists (finite-dependence on \(\mathbb Z^2\)) | **proved** |
| Width-\(W\) density \(a_W=(W-h_{\text{row}}^{(W)})/(W-1)\), Blackwell/strip transfer | **proved** (via m11g) |
| \(a_W\to a_{2D}\) as \(W\to\infty\) | **standard + numerical** (no rate proved) |
| MC filter reproduces enumeration (\(W=2,3\)) | **witnessed** (\(\sim10^{-3}\)) |

**Non-claims (stand):** not continuum \(L(\rho,g)\); not \(dS_c/d\tau\); not gravity. \(a\) is a discrete per-site entropy-production density for a 2D classical lattice.

**Open (final rung):** the **continuum embedding** — a scaling limit sending the discrete density to a continuum entropy-production density (the bridge to the load term \(\gamma\lvert dS_c/d\tau\rvert\)), or a precise obstruction.
